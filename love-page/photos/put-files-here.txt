PUT YOUR PHOTOS AND VIDEOS HERE 📸
====================================

This folder is where all the images for the page live.

1. Drop your photos here. Supported: .jpg, .jpeg, .png (keep them under ~2MB each)

2. VIDEOS are supported too! Drop a short clip here as `vid1.mp4`
   (or .webm). It will autoplay muted and loop in the big media tile -
   perfect for a memory clip. Keep clips short and small (<10MB) so the
   page loads fast. Supported: .mp4, .webm

3. Open index.html and find the "Our Memories" section (search for
   "add your photo here"). Each placeholder has instructions right above
   it. Replace them with <img> tags, like:

     <img src="photos/my-photo.jpg"
          style="width:100%;height:100%;object-fit:cover;border-radius:20px;"
          alt="a memory of us">

   The video tile already points at photos/vid1.mp4 -just drop your
   file there (or change the src attribute to your own filename).

Tip: to make sure your photos aren't rotated weirdly, use straight
vertical photos for the square tiles and a wide/landscape photo for the
big featured tile.
