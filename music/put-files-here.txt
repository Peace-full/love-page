PUT YOUR MUSIC HERE 🎵
======================

This folder is where the background music files live.

1. Drop your songs here. Supported: .mp3, .ogg, .wav, .m4a

2. Open main.js and add each file to the PLAYLIST list at the top:

     const PLAYLIST = [
       "music/Your Song 1.mp3",
       "music/Your Song 2.mp3",
     ];

   One file per line, exact filename (spaces are fine).

3. The page auto-plays the first song after the visitor taps the
   music disc / anywhere on the page, and loops through the list.

   NOTE: songs with long intros might not autoplay perfectly on phones
   (mobile browsers block sound until the user taps -that's expected).
